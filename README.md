# Structural Truss FEA & Vibration Analysis

## Overview
Modeled a multi-member structural truss in **SolidWorks** based on specified geometric constraints and performed both **Static Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** and **Modal (Frequency) Vibration Analysis** using **SolidWorks Simulation**. The study evaluated structural integrity, stress concentrations, and dynamic behavior under applied external loads.

## Key Results
* **Deformation & Bending Behavior:** Displacement animations revealed inward center-bending deflection across the primary span under peak loading.
* **Stress Distribution:** Evaluated Von Mises stress across truss members and joints to verify factors of safety against material yield limits.
* **Vibration & Natural Frequencies:** Conducted modal analysis to identify resonant frequencies and mode shapes to ensure dynamic stability.
